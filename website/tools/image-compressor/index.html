Image compressor

Make photos and graphics smaller without uploading them anywhere. Choose a format and quality, optionally limit the width, compare the file sizes and download the result.

How to use the Image Compressor

  1. Drop an image onto the box or click Choose image.
  2. Pick WebP or JPEG for the smallest files, or PNG to keep every pixel.
  3. Lower the quality slider, or set a maximum width, until the preview still looks good.
  4. Check the size saving and click Download.

What it is good for

  • Making website images smaller so pages load faster.
  • Reducing photos to fit email attachment and form upload limits.
  • Resizing large camera images to a sensible width.
  • Converting between JPEG, PNG and WebP.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image is decoded, resized and re-encoded by your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

Which format gives the smallest file?

WebP is usually smallest, then JPEG for photos. PNG is lossless, so it is often larger and ignores the quality slider.

Why did my file get bigger?

Re-encoding an already well-compressed image, or converting a JPEG to PNG, can increase the size. Lower the quality, reduce the width or choose WebP.

Is metadata such as EXIF removed?

Yes. Drawing the image onto a canvas drops camera metadata, including location, which is useful before sharing a photo.

Is transparency kept?

PNG and WebP keep transparency. JPEG has none, so transparent areas become white.
