    How to use the Regex Tester

    1. Type your pattern in the Pattern box, without the surrounding slashes.
    2. Tick the flags you need. g finds all matches, i ignores case.
    3. Paste your text. Matches are highlighted and listed with their position and groups.

    What it is good for

    • Building and debugging patterns before putting them in code.
    • Extracting parts of log lines and URLs with capture groups.
    • Checking validation patterns against sample input.
    • Learning regex by experimenting.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which regex flavour does it use?

    JavaScript (ECMAScript), the engine built into your browser. Patterns written for PCRE, Python or .NET may need small changes, for example around lookbehind or named group syntax.

    What do the flags mean?

    g finds all matches, i ignores case, m makes ^ and $ match at line breaks, s lets . match newlines, and u enables full Unicode matching.

    Why did the page freeze?

    Some patterns, such as nested quantifiers like (a+)+, can take exponential time on certain input, called catastrophic backtracking. Simplify the pattern or limit the test text.

    Is my text uploaded?

    No. Matching runs entirely in your browser.
