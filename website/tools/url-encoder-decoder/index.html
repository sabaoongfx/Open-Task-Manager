URL encoder and decoder

Percent-encode text for use in a URL, or decode a messy encoded link back into readable text. Pick component mode for query values or full-URL mode for whole links.

How to use the URL Encoder and Decoder

  1. Choose a mode: encode a component, encode a full URL, or decode.
  2. Type or paste your text. The result appears immediately.
  3. Click Copy output to use it.

What it is good for

  • Building query strings with special characters, spaces and non-English text.
  • Reading tracking links and redirects full of %20 and %3D.
  • Debugging API calls where a parameter gets mangled.
  • Preparing mailto: and share links.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between component and full URL mode?

Component mode (encodeURIComponent) escapes everything that is not a letter, digit or a few safe symbols, including / ? & and =, which is right for a single parameter value. Full URL mode (encodeURI) leaves those structural characters alone so a whole link stays usable.

Why does a space become %20 or +?

Percent-encoding writes a space as %20. In HTML form submissions a space is sometimes written as +, so decoding offers an option to treat + as a space.

Why can't it decode my text?

A % sign that isn't followed by two hex digits is invalid. Fix or remove the stray % and try again.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Encoding and decoding use your browser's built-in functions on your device.
