    How to use the Word Counter

    1. Type or paste text into the box.
    2. Read the counts, which update as you type.
    3. Check the top keywords to see which words you repeat.

    What it is good for

    • Keeping essays, posts and abstracts within a word limit.
    • Checking meta descriptions and social posts against character limits.
    • Estimating how long an article takes to read or a speech takes to deliver.
    • Spotting overused words.

    Frequently asked questions

    How are words counted?

    A word is a run of letters or digits, which may include apostrophes and hyphens. This matches how most word processors count, though small differences with other tools are normal.

    How is reading time calculated?

    At about 225 words per minute for silent reading and 130 words per minute for speaking, rounded to whole minutes.

    Does it count characters with spaces?

    Both are shown: total characters including spaces, and characters without spaces.

    Is my text saved?

    No. It is processed live in your browser and is gone when you close the tab.
